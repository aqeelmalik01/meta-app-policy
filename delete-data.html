This page explains how to request deletion of information associated with this application.

Request Deletion

Send an email to YOUR_EMAIL_ADDRESS with the subject Facebook App Data Deletion Request. Include enough information to identify the relevant account or Page connection.

After the request is verified, applicable information stored by the application will be deleted.

Remove Facebook Access

You may also remove the application's access from your Facebook account or Meta business settings. Removing access prevents the application from making future API requests using that authorization.
